Dhanshiri: A Journey from 2019

Dhanshiri was built, funded, and closely supervised by the owner, M A Wadud Miah. It opened for rentals in 2020. Since then, 5 families have lived here.

Dhanshiri is a comfortable solitude from the bustle of Shathibari bazar, yet not too far from it. The greenery around, pleasing paddy fields, and the ponds of Fish Seed Multiplication Farm provide a calm environment to reside in.
